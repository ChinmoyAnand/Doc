# Privacy Policy for AdvoCase

**Last updated: February 22, 2026**

## Introduction

AdvoCase ("we", "our", or "us") is a legal case management application developed by Chinmoy Patra. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application ("App"), available on Android and iOS.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy is intended to help you understand what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a. Information You Provide
- Case details, hearing notes, and other legal diary entries you create within the App
- Your name and email address when you register or are invited to join a firm account

### b. Device Permissions We Request

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Camera** (`android.permission.CAMERA`) | To capture photos of documents, evidence, or case-related materials and attach them to case records |
| **Read/Write External Storage** | To save and access attachments (photos, documents) associated with your cases |
| **Internet** | To sync case data with our secure backend (Supabase) and send email notifications |

### c. Automatically Collected Information
We do **not** collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash telemetry beyond what is strictly necessary to operate the App.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide core app functionality: creating and managing legal case records, hearings, and attachments
- To enable firm/team collaboration features (inviting colleagues, shared case access)
- To send email notifications related to case updates or invitations

---

## 3. Data Storage

- **Case data** is stored securely in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database hosted on cloud infrastructure.
- **Attachments** (photos and documents) are stored **locally on your device** and are not uploaded to any cloud storage.
- Passwords are stored in our database and are never shared with third parties.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share data only in the following circumstances:
- **Service providers**: We use Supabase for database hosting and Resend for transactional email delivery. These providers process data only as necessary to deliver the service.
- **Legal requirements**: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

---

## 5. In-App Purchases

AdvoCase offers subscription plans ("AdvoCase Pro") through the Google Play Store and Apple App Store, processed via RevenueCat. We do not collect or store your payment information. All billing is handled by the respective app store.

---

## 6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

AdvoCase is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## 8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion of your data
- Object to or restrict processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.
